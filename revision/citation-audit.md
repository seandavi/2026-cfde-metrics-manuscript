# Claim–citation fidelity audit (#85)

Audit of every citation, and every uncited claim that carries a number or an attribution, in `content/05.cfde-metrics-paper.md` (main text, figure legends, Resources), `content/boxes/*.md` and `content/supplement.md`, as they stood on `revision-1` at e073f7b. It uses only our text and public sources.

## Method

- **Locations** are section and paragraph, with the line number in `content/05.cfde-metrics-paper.md` at e073f7b (one sentence per line). Each row quotes the sentence as it was before this audit.
- **Sources** were read on 2026-11-04: Crossref and DataCite metadata and abstracts; Europe PMC and NCBI E-utilities for PubMed abstracts and PMC full text; Zenodo, arXiv and publisher pages for open full text; and the cited web pages themselves. No request carried an email address or `mailto` parameter, and Unpaywall was not used.
- **Verdicts.** SUPPORTED: the source says it. PARTLY: overstated, wrong number, wrong scope, misquoted, or the right source at the wrong URL. UNSUPPORTED: the source does not make the claim. UNVERIFIABLE: no access to the text, or the claim names no source.
- Where a sentence carries several citations, each gets its own row; ″ repeats the sentence above.

Either may be where the figure came from.
